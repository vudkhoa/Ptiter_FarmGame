## TinyWorlds - boiling water loops

Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/boiling-water-loops

Direct source file:
https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/cooking_with_cover_01.ogg

- `sfx_cooking_sizzle_loop.ogg` - renamed from `cooking_with_cover_01.ogg`
  (4.86 s, seamless loop)

The source page identifies the work as CC0. For the short cooking animation,
play roughly 0.65-1.20 seconds of this loop and fade or stop it when the lid
lands; keeping the source as a loop also supports longer recipes later.
